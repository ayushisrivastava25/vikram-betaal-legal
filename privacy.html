Privacy policy

Updated 12 November 2026 · Vikram Betaal website, iPhone/iPad and Android apps

Ayushi Srivastava publishes Vikram Betaal and is responsible for the game's handling of your information. Privacy contact: ayushis2k10@gmail.com.

Information used to run the game

  • Account information: account identifiers, username, optional journey name, sign-in provider, and information that Apple or Google supplies when you choose their sign-in service, such as an email address or name. Apple may supply a private relay address.
  • Your journey: Katha answers, results, progress, completion times and account preferences. These are saved in the cloud so you can resume on another device.
  • Support: feedback you send through the game, or messages you email to support, and the identifiers needed to associate your request with your account.
  • Technical information: connection and security logs used by our hosting and account services, which may include an IP address and device/browser information.

Username passwords are handled by the authentication service. The mobile apps keep your signed-in session in protected device storage, not your password. Essential website session cookies maintain sign-in. Gameplay needs an internet connection; there is no offline save queue.

Temporary play

Before sign-in, the first tale uses a temporary cloud session lasting up to 24 hours. Sign in to attach it to your account. The apps keep that temporary session in memory, so closing the app can lose access to it. You can delete it in Privacy choices → Temporary journey.

Optional analytics

Analytics are off until you allow them. If enabled, PostHog and Google Analytics for Firebase receive a random analytics identifier, app/device identifiers, device and app information, and events such as screens visited, tales completed, sharing, sign-in and settings actions. Google Analytics may infer approximate location from network information. The game does not request GPS location.

Our game-event payloads exclude passwords, usernames, email addresses, written feedback, answer text and personality scores. The random analytics identifier is associated with your game account in our backend. Advertising features and session recordings are disabled.

Change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices → Analytics. Disabling analytics stops new optional collection; it does not automatically erase earlier records. To request deletion of historical analytics, contact us through in-game Feedback before deleting your account, so we can associate the request with its analytics identifier.

Notifications and sharing

Mobile reminders require notification permission. They are scheduled locally on your device; no remote push token is sent to a game server. You can disable them in game or device settings. Signing out cancels reminders on that device; manage other devices separately.

Sharing creates a temporary image and opens your device's share sheet. You choose the destination. Your username is excluded unless you opt in; the Share app card does not include player information. Shared copies are controlled by you and the chosen destination.

Service providers and processing locations

We use Supabase for cloud accounts and saved journeys; PostHog and Google Analytics for Firebase for optional analytics; Apple and Google for their sign-in services; and OpenAI Sites/Cloudflare for website hosting. These services may process information outside India. Their own policies also describe their handling of information:

  • Supabase privacy policy
  • PostHog privacy policy
  • Google privacy policy
  • Apple privacy policy
  • OpenAI privacy policy and Cloudflare privacy policy

Retention and deletion

Saved account and journey records remain until the account is deleted. Account deletion removes the cloud account, game results, preferences and in-game feedback. Temporary cloud sessions expire after up to 24 hours. Backup copies may remain until the provider's backup retention expires.

Historical analytics have separate service retention settings and are not automatically erased by disabling analytics or deleting a game account. Support email correspondence is separate from in-game feedback. Contact us for deletion requests concerning analytics, support email or a separate legacy website account.

Use Settings → Delete account in the app, or follow the account and data deletion instructions. We may need to verify ownership before carrying out an emailed request. Never send your password.

Younger players

The stories include supernatural imagery and moral dilemmas. Check the age rating shown in your local store. A content rating does not replace applicable rules for children's personal information. A parent or guardian who has concerns about a child's account can contact ayushis2k10@gmail.com.

Your choices and questions

You can change analytics and sharing choices, disable reminders, correct your journey name, or request access, correction or deletion by contacting ayushis2k10@gmail.com. Available rights depend on applicable law. We will update this page when our practices change.
