How can we help?

Support for your journey through the forest.

Contact the publisher

Vikram Betaal is published by Ayushi Srivastava. For gameplay, sign-in, privacy or account-deletion help, email ayushis2k10@gmail.com.

Email support

Report a problem

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version/build, the Katha or screen involved, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Never send your password, sign-in tokens or verification codes.

You can also use Settings → Feedback inside the game.

Return to your journey

Use the same sign-in method and account you used before. Apple, Google and username sign-ins may lead to different journeys. An internet connection is required to load and save progress.

Username account help

Username accounts do not have a verified recovery email. Contact support if you cannot sign in; recovery depends on being able to verify account ownership.
