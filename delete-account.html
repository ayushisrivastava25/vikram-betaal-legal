Account & data deletion

Delete your Vikram Betaal account in the app, or contact the publisher without reinstalling it.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete. For an Apple account, use Continue with Apple.
  2. Open Settings → Delete account within 15 minutes of signing in.
  3. Read the confirmation, type DELETE, and choose Permanently delete account.

Deletion cannot be undone. Your cloud account, saved Katha results, preferences and in-game feedback are removed. Reminders are cancelled on the current mobile device; turn them off separately on other devices.

Request deletion without the app

Email ayushis2k10@gmail.com with the subject Vikram Betaal account deletion. Include your username or journey name, the sign-in method you used, and the email associated with Google or Apple if known. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, mention that. Tell us whether you want the entire account deleted or only specific data.

Request deletion by email

We may ask for information to verify that you own the account. Do not send passwords, sign-in tokens or verification codes.

Other data

For historical analytics deletion, use in-game Feedback before deleting the account so we can associate the request with its analytics identifier. Optional analytics and support email correspondence are not automatically erased with the game account. A separate legacy website account also needs a specific request. Mention these in your message if relevant.

Backups may retain deleted records until the provider's retention expires. Copies of cards you shared are managed by you and the service you shared them to.
